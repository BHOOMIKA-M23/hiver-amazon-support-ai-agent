HIVER AMAZON SUPPORT AGENT
REPLY QUALITY EVALUATION RUBRIC

Each generated reply is evaluated on the following dimensions.

1. REPLY QUALITY — HUMAN SCORE

Score 2 = Good
- Directly addresses the customer's request.
- Helpful and understandable.
- Professional and concise.
- Appropriate for Amazon customer support.

Score 1 = Partially good
- Somewhat addresses the request.
- Useful but incomplete, vague, or slightly awkward.
- Does not contain a major harmful error.

Score 0 = Poor
- Does not address the customer's request.
- Gives incorrect or unsupported information.
- Is misleading, unsafe, or clearly unhelpful.

2. EVIDENCE SUPPORT — HUMAN LABEL

supported
- The reply is supported by the retrieved historical examples.
- The reply does not introduce important unsupported facts.

partially_supported
- Most of the reply is supported, but some detail is not clearly supported.

unsupported
- The reply contains an important claim, instruction, policy, URL,
  or action that is not supported by the historical evidence.

3. LLM-AS-JUDGE

The judge receives:
- Customer message
- Retrieved historical evidence
- Generated reply

The judge must independently score:

reply_quality:
0 = Poor
1 = Partially good
2 = Good

evidence_supported:
supported
partially_supported
unsupported

The judge must also provide a short reason.

4. HUMAN VS JUDGE AGREEMENT

For reply quality:
- Exact agreement = human score equals judge score.
- Report agreement percentage.

For evidence support:
- Exact agreement = human evidence label equals judge evidence label.
- Report agreement percentage.

5. IMPORTANT EVALUATION RULE

The judge must evaluate whether the generated reply is actually supported
by the retrieved evidence, not whether the reply merely sounds plausible.

A fluent answer containing invented information must receive a poor
evidence-support rating.

6. HUMAN REVIEW PROCEDURE

For each example:
1. Read the customer message.
2. Read the historical evidence.
3. Read the generated reply.
4. Assign human reply-quality score.
5. Assign human evidence-support label.
6. Do not change the labels after seeing the judge's prediction.

7. LIMITATION

The evaluation uses a small held-out set of 30 examples.

Therefore, reply-quality and judge-agreement results should be reported
as evidence on this evaluation sample, not as a claim about all Amazon
customer-support conversations.
