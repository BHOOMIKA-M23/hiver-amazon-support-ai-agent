DECISION LOGIC TEST RESULTS
===========================

The escalation decision system was tested using five representative scenarios.

Test 1: Strong refund case
Customer: I want to return this item and get a refund.
Expected: auto_handle
Actual: auto_handle
Result: PASS
Reason: Relevant historical evidence is available and no escalation trigger was detected.

Test 2: Weak evidence
Customer: I have a problem with something I ordered.
Expected: escalate
Actual: escalate
Result: PASS
Reason: Insufficient historical evidence for a confident reply.

Test 3: Explicit human request
Customer: My package is late. I want to speak to a human agent.
Expected: escalate
Actual: escalate
Result: PASS
Reason: Customer explicitly requested human support.

Test 4: Account/payment issue
Customer: There is an unexpected charge on my account.
Expected: escalate
Actual: escalate
Result: PASS
Reason: Account or payment issues require human review.

Test 5: Strong delivery case
Customer: My package says delivered but I never received it.
Expected: auto_handle
Actual: auto_handle
Result: PASS
Reason: Relevant historical evidence is available and no escalation trigger was detected.

Summary:
5 out of 5 decision tests passed.

The tests confirm that the system can:
- Auto-handle cases with relevant historical evidence.
- Escalate when historical evidence is weak.
- Escalate when the customer explicitly requests human support.
- Escalate account and payment issues for human review.
