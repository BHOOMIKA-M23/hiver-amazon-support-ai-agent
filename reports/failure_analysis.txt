TOP 5 FAILURE MODES
===================

1. OTHER vs SPECIFIC INTENT CONFUSION

Example:
Actual: other
Predicted: refund_return

Message:
"So basically, I just want an email address or a postal address that I can submit a formal complaint to, please."

Hypothesis:
The classifier relies heavily on words such as "submit" and may associate complaint-related language with refund/return examples. The "other" category is also broad and contains many different types of messages.

Potential improvement:
Add more diverse examples to the other category and use stronger intent definitions or a semantic classifier.


2. DELIVERY ISSUES MISCLASSIFIED AS OTHER

Example:
Actual: delivery_shipping
Predicted: other

Message:
"What is this?? Order still not delivered for yours kind information."

Hypothesis:
Short delivery complaints contain limited vocabulary. When messages do not contain strong words such as "package", "tracking", or "delivery", the TF-IDF model may fail to recognize the delivery intent.

Potential improvement:
Add more short delivery examples and use semantic embeddings instead of relying only on word overlap.


3. PRODUCT TROUBLESHOOTING MISCLASSIFIED

Example:
Actual: product_help
Predicted: other

Message:
"Our home screen won't load"

Hypothesis:
Very short troubleshooting messages contain few distinctive terms. The model does not have enough lexical evidence to identify product_help reliably.

Potential improvement:
Add more product/device troubleshooting examples and use an embedding-based classifier or LLM classification.


4. OVERLAPPING ORDER, DELIVERY AND REFUND LANGUAGE

Example:
Actual: order_issue
Predicted: refund_return

Message:
"Made a purchase at the same time for 2 items Mon. Notified 1 was shipped, went to track the other and it said 'this was cancelled'."

Hypothesis:
Customer-support intents naturally overlap. Words such as order, shipped, cancelled, payment and return can appear across multiple categories.

Potential improvement:
Define clearer boundary rules and use semantic classification with examples for each intent.


5. PRIME AND MULTILINGUAL MESSAGES ARE HARD TO CLASSIFY

Example 1:
Actual: prime_issue
Predicted: other

Message:
"None of those solutions worked, unfortunately. And now none of the Prime movies have video."

Example 2:
Actual: account_payment
Predicted: other

Message:
"ギフト券使ってないんてますけど、支払いってギフト券でしか払えないんですか？"

Hypothesis:
The training set contains relatively few Prime and multilingual examples. The TF-IDF model therefore has limited vocabulary coverage for these cases.

Potential improvement:
Add more Prime and multilingual training examples and use a multilingual semantic model or LLM-based classification.


OVERALL OBSERVATION
===================

The classifier achieved 46.7% accuracy and 0.21 macro F1 on the 30-example test set.

The main limitation is the small manually labelled dataset of 150 examples. Several intents have very few examples, while "other" is the largest category. TF-IDF + Logistic Regression is therefore sensitive to vocabulary overlap and struggles with short, ambiguous, multilingual, and overlapping customer-support messages.
